ABOUT ME

(Dr.) Geraj Vinoth Wilfer is one of the most trusted lawyers for handling confidential legal transactions. He provides transparent and systematic case management at a reasonable fee.

He and his associates, based in Tirunelveli, Chennai, and Madurai, manage complex documentation related to litigation, as well as personal and business transactions.

He offers legal opinions for dispute avoidance, property matters, commercial transactions, and marital disputes, including criminal complaints. He also collaborates with advocates from Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Kochi, and Jaipur to provide clients with comprehensive legal support.

Clients from abroad and outside Tamil Nadu trust him for his honest and reliable handling of critical documentation and legal opinions. His charges are based on a detailed, item-wise billing system, as per the disclosed fee structure. He ensures regular updates, clear strategies, and consistent follow-up throughout each case.

He also offers a free initial consultation upon receiving the relevant documents and client ID proof. Dr. Wilfer is proficient in Tamil, English, and Hindi, spoken and written, at a professional level.
